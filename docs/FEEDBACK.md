# SocialScope feedback and issues

Open **Feedback / Report an issue** in the sidebar while signed in. Choose **Feedback** or **Issue**, enter a message (up to 5,000 characters), and submit. A confirmation means the report was saved. A failed request preserves your text for retry.

Describe what you expected and what happened. Your account, organization, current page path, and submission time are attached automatically; URL query strings and fragments are omitted. Do not include passwords, provider keys, tokens, private research data, or sensitive post content.

Reports are visible only to authorized administrators in your organization. Administrators open **Admin ? Feedback & issues**, read the message, and toggle **Reviewed**. There are no public reports, attachments, replies, notifications, or user-facing status pages.

For a login/access problem, contact your administrator directly because the form requires authentication. Account invitations and email password recovery are not included.
